 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

 Executive Summary
An interactive end-to-end Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, regional revenue distribution, customer buying behavior, and product categories for the Superstore dataset.

# 🔑 Key Insights & Business Questions Answered
1. **Sales Trend & Seasonality:** Monthly revenue distribution to track growth trajectories.
2. **Regional Performance:** Identification of top-performing regions and states for targeted marketing.
3. **Customer Segment Analysis:** Breaking down purchasing behavior across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
4. **Top 10 Products & Customers:** Identifying core revenue drivers and key loyalty accounts.


## 🛠️ Excel Features Used
* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts:** Dynamic aggregation of underlying transactional data.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Multi-connected filters (Region, Category, Year) via Report Connections.
* **Data Visualization Best Practices:** Customized horizontal bar charts,  and trendlines.
